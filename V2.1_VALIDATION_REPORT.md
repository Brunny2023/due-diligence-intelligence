## Scope of the hardening release

V2.1 preserves the V2 architecture and product positioning. It removes arbitrary default numerical risk scores, adds evidence-calibrated qualitative dimension assessments, separates evidence quality from risk severity, and ensures that the Executive Decision Snapshot is derived from structured findings carrying the same verification status used in the detailed analysis and Diligence Decision Map.

## Remaining limitations

The Skill remains evidence-bounded and document-first. It cannot independently prove a claim without supplied or explicitly registered evidence; it does not perform legal, financial, investment, tax, accounting, or regulatory advice; it does not calculate a financial score absent documented, reproducible inputs; and it must not be read as a recommendation or final transaction conclusion.
